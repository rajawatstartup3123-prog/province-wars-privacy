Privacy Policy

Province Wars: Conquer the World. Developer: Krishna Singh. App ID: com.krishnasingh.provincewars. Last updated: 10 November 2026.

The short version

Province Wars is a single-player strategy game. There are no accounts and no sign-in. Your campaign is saved only on your device. We do not run servers that store your progress.

Information the game collects

Nothing that leaves your device. Your commander name, chosen country, progress, coins, upgrades and settings are stored locally so the game can remember them. We do not upload this information and we cannot see it.

Advertising

The game may show ads (banners and optional rewarded ads) using Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID, IP address and basic device and app-usage data to show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK) you are asked for consent first. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings > Google > Ads. See How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

In-app purchases

The game offers optional one-time war packs (in-game coins and shields) sold through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment. The game receives only a purchase confirmation so it can deliver the pack. We never receive your name, card or payment details. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Information we share

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information.

Permissions

The game does not use the camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos or files. Internet access is used only for Google Play purchases and for ads. Gameplay works offline.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Your campaign and settings exist only on your device. Clearing the app's data in Android Settings, or uninstalling the app, removes them permanently.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page and the date above will be revised.
